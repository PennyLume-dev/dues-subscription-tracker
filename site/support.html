Common questions

Is Dues really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no ads, no account and no limit on how many subscriptions you track.

Why is a price different from what I pay?

Catalog prices are verified for the US and India. Elsewhere they're estimates converted from US prices. Tap a subscription, then Edit, to set exactly what you pay. Taxes and promotions can also change the final amount.

How do I change my currency?

Settings → Region → pick your country. Saved prices are converted automatically at the current exchange rate.

I'm not getting reminders

Open Settings → Notifications, turn reminders on and allow notifications for Dues. Use Send test notification to check. Some phones also need Dues excluded from battery optimisation.

How do I cancel a subscription I'm tracking?

Dues tracks subscriptions but can't cancel them for you. Cancel with the provider (or in your app store's subscriptions page), then tap Mark as cancelled in Dues.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your phone. Use Settings → Data → Delete all subscriptions, or uninstall the app. We don't keep a copy.
