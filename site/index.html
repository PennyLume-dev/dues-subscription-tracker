FAQ

Questions about Dues

What is Dues?

Dues is a free Android app that tracks all your subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify, ChatGPT, iCloud+ and 65 more), shows what you really spend each month and year, and reminds you before every renewal or free-trial charge.

Is Dues free?

Yes. Dues is completely free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no limit on how many subscriptions you track.

Does Dues need an account or see my data?

No. Dues works without an account. Your subscriptions stay on your phone and are never uploaded; you can export them to CSV for backups.

Which countries have verified subscription prices?

Dues has verified local prices for the United States, India, the United Kingdom, Germany, France, Canada, Australia, Singapore, the UAE, Brazil, Japan and Mexico. Other countries see prices converted from US dollars at live exchange rates.

Can Dues cancel subscriptions for me?

No. Dues reminds you before you are charged, but you cancel with the provider (or your app store), then mark it as cancelled in Dues.

Is Dues open source?

Yes. The Android app is open source under the GPL-3.0 license on GitHub.
