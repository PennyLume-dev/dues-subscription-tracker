Support Dues

Dues is free: no ads, no purchases, no account. It's built by Pennylume, a tiny independent studio. If Dues saves you money, here's how you can help it grow.

Free ways to help

  • Star Dues on GitHub. Stars help other people find it: github.com/PennyLume-dev/dues-subscription-tracker
  • Share it with a friend who has too many subscriptions.
  • Report a wrong price or a missing service through GitHub issues or SUPPORT_EMAIL.

Send a tip

Tips are optional and unlock nothing: every feature stays free for everyone. They go toward publishing Dues on Google Play and building the next Pennylume apps.

Tip options are opening soon. Until then, a GitHub star means a lot.
