Privacy Policy

App: Dues – Subscription Tracker · Last updated: 26 September 2026

Dues helps you keep track of the subscriptions you pay for. We built it so your data stays on your phone. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

Summary

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • Your subscriptions, prices, notes and settings are stored only on your device.
  • We do not run analytics or advertising, and we do not sell or share personal data.
  • Dues is free. There are no purchases, ads or accounts.

Data stored on your device

The subscriptions you add (name, price, billing cycle, payment date, list, category, the payment-method label you type, website, notes and price history) and your preferences (country, currency, reminder settings). This data never leaves your device, except when you export it to a file yourself. Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Data → Delete all subscriptions removes it.

Network requests the app makes

To work, the app contacts these services. Like any internet request, they can see your device's IP address. None of them receives your subscription list, prices or notes.

  • Price list updates – dues-app.vercel.app, to download the latest subscription prices. Nothing about you or your subscriptions is sent.
  • Exchange rates – open.er-api.com, to show prices in your currency. Only a general request for current rates is sent.
  • Brand logos – img.logo.dev (Logo.dev) and Google's favicon service, to show service logos. The website domain of a service (for example netflix.com) is sent to fetch its logo.

Notifications

Renewal reminders are scheduled and shown on your device. No notification service or server is involved.

Permissions

  • Notifications – only to show the renewal reminders you turn on.
  • Internet – for the requests listed above.

Children

Dues is intended for adults managing their own spending and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any subscription, turn reminders off, export your data as CSV, or delete everything at any time from inside the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
