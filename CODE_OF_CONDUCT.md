# Code of Conduct

Dues follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind are not accepted.

Report problems privately to pennylume@proton.me. Reports are handled confidentially.
